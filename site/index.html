Drive your coding agents
from anywhere.

Iron Rain is a native Apple app that lets you start, steer, and approve claude-code and opencode sessions running on your Mac — from your iPhone, on your subscription, end-to-end encrypted.

Join the TestFlight View on GitHub

Install

1 The Mac daemon — one line (needs Go + git):

curl -fsSL https://howlerops.github.io/oculus/install.sh | sh

Builds oculusd into ~/.local/bin. Start it with oculusd serve --secret <your-secret> — it auto-detects opencode, claude-code, and pi, then prints a pairing QR.

2 The iOS app — join the TestFlight beta, then scan the QR to pair.

Projects & worktrees

Spawn sessions in any project folder, or in an isolated git worktree on its own branch — with per-project setup hooks that copy your .env, install deps, and assign a port. Review the diff and open a PR when you're done.

Approve from your pocket

Tool calls block until you approve them — with a real Allow / Deny you can answer from the lock screen. Live Activities show what the agent is doing in real time.

Multiple Macs, grouped

Pair every Mac you own, name them, and switch between them instantly. Your sessions stay grouped by machine and project.

Your keys, your subscription

claude-code runs on your Claude subscription. Traffic is end-to-end encrypted between phone and Mac — Iron Rain never sees your code or your keys.

How it works

  1. Run the Iron Rain daemon on your Mac (it auto-detects opencode, claude-code, and pi).
  2. Scan the pairing QR from the iOS app.
  3. Start a session in any project — approve tools, review worktree diffs, ship.
